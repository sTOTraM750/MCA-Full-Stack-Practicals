BEGINNER NOTES:
- <img> embeds an image. src gives its path; alt describes it; width and height specify dimensions.
- <a> creates a hyperlink. href="#gallery" jumps to the element whose id is gallery.
- target="_blank" requests a new tab/window; rel="noopener noreferrer" adds protective link behavior.
- <div> groups related content.
- id identifies a particular element; class is reusable across elements.
- CSS .class selects by class; #id selects by ID.
- padding is inner spacing; margin is outer spacing.
- display:inline-block allows cards to sit beside one another.
- object-fit:cover fills the image area and may crop it.
- :hover applies styles while the pointer is over a link.
- @media applies styles based on viewport conditions.
